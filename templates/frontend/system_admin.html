Create Employee Account
System Administrator only · Student accounts are automatically generated during enrollment.
Automatic security: The system generates the temporary password. It is not entered manually. The account starts as Pending Verification and an activation email containing the login email, temporary password, and verification link is sent to the registered email.
Edit Employee Account
Update employee information, role, or account status.
This account is locked after repeated failed sign-ins. Unlock it to restore access, or issue a temporary password for someone who also forgot the one they had.
Edit User Roles
Tick every role this person should hold. A user can be, for example, a Teacher and a Principal at the same time.

Roles are written to the database immediately. The user's access updates on their next request, and the change is recorded in the audit log for compliance.
Create Virtual Classroom
Links a subject, section, grade level, teacher, and Academic Year into one instructional unit. Applied/Core classrooms auto-enroll the section's students and pre-populate the topic outline.
Manage Students in Class
Add or remove learners in the section's cohort for this Virtual Classroom.
Assign Section
Sets the student's section for the active school year and syncs their Virtual Classroom enrollments (adds new, drops stale ones).
Post Announcement
School-wide announcements appear on every dashboard until archived.
New School Year
The new year is created as the active school year. The previous active year is automatically archived, since the institution runs one academic year at a time. Choose the grading structure — it governs how assessments and grades are computed for the whole year.
Add Section
A section organises students within a grade level for the active school year.
Assign Students — Section
