Business Rules
  • At the start of a new Academic Year, each student's grade level auto-increments only if Final Grade ≥ 75 in ALL learning areas (DepEd).
  • Non-passers are NOT auto-incremented; they are flagged for retention / remedial.
  • Grade 6 and Grade 12 passers graduate and are marked Alumni.
  • A Grade 6 passer continuing to JHS receives a dual record: Alumni (Elementary) + new Grade 7 record (High School).

Before You Import
Your CSV or Excel file must contain the following columns in order:
LRN | Last Name | First Name | Grade Level | Section | Subject Applied
Row 1 must be the header. Each row represents one subject applied by a student — a student may apply for multiple subjects. LRN must be exactly 12 digits.
